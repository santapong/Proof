# Standards this skill applies

Graded on the plugin's three levels — **Yes** (ratified by a standards body), **Draft**
(real working-group output, unratified), **No** (real and widely followed, still
somebody's opinion). The grade is provenance of authority, not quality. This shelf is
thinner on "Yes" than the engineering skills' shelves by the nature of the domain:
product and go-to-market method is practitioner canon, not ratified standard — which is
exactly why the cite-or-own rule (`lifecycle.md` §5) matters more here, not less.

| Authority | Version / edition | Grade | What this skill takes from it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jobs-to-be-Done (Christensen et al.) | *Competing Against Luck* (2016) framing | No — practitioner theory | Discovery's persona-as-context-plus-job rule and the workaround-as-competitor lens (`discovery.md` §1) |
| Continuous Discovery (Torres) | *Continuous Discovery Habits* (2021) | No — practitioner method | Evidence-over-opinion discipline; the interview frame researchers apply to found evidence |
| Lean Startup / riskiest-assumption testing (Ries; Croll & Yoskovitz) | *The Lean Startup* (2011), *Lean Analytics* (2013) | No — practitioner canon | GATE-2's kill/pivot/proceed structure and the cheapest-honest-validation rule (`vision-roadmap.md` §3) |
| Positioning (Dunford) | *Obviously Awesome* (2019) | No — practitioner method | The competitive-alternative-first ordering and the differentiator swap-test (`go-to-market.md` §1) |
| Pricing (Simon-Kucher school; *Monetizing Innovation*, Ramanujam & Tacke) | 2016 edition | No — practitioner method | Model-before-numbers, value-axis charging, and willingness-to-pay anchoring (`go-to-market.md` §1) |
| SemVer | **2.0.0** | No — de-facto specification | The meaning of P4's v1 line, cited through `loop-build`'s shelf, which owns it |
| ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207 | 2017 edition | Yes — ratified, published | The sanity frame that a lifecycle is processes with defined outcomes; the venture graph's phase-gate-deliverable shape is consistent with its process view |
| LangGraph state model | as restated in `loop-context/references/shared-state.md` | No — engineering design, internal restatement | The typed-state, per-field-reducer, checkpoint discipline the venture state contract instantiates |
